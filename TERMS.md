# MedLit product boundary

MedLit is educational decision support, not peer review, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or a determination that a study is true or clinically applicable. The complete public terms live at `/terms`.

Users are responsible for provider charges, protecting credentials, lawful access to submitted content, and verifying outputs against the source. MedLit does not bypass paywalls.

The September 13 local review draft expands company capacity and protected persons, proposes a collective liability cap and limited organizational indemnity, preserves non-waivable rights, and defines sensitive-data permissions and prospective changes. The cap is a commercial drafting proposal, not an effective clause or statutory safe harbor. No binding arbitration or exclusive venue is imposed. The new local draft is distinct from the September 13 public deployment.

See [the legal research and editable drafts](docs/legal/README.md). Exact entity, mailing address, monitored contact, audience, enforceability and operating procedures remain unresolved. A draft label does not suspend duties to current visitors.

Owner/counsel review is required before promoting the revised legal notice: confirm the contracting legal entity, monitored contact, institutional/minor-use policy and actual backend configuration. Sources consulted include California Civil Code 1668, the California Attorney General's CCPA guidance, and Firebase's Google/Apple authentication documentation. These drafts are product-grounded terms, not a legal opinion about enforceability.

Applicability diligence also remains open for health-related inferences from searches/reading histories, consumer-health privacy and breach-notification rules, and EU lawful bases/transfers/special-category data if in scope. Discussing medical literature does not by itself settle HIPAA or consumer-health-law applicability. App Store release must verify both a reachable policy and actual in-app account deletion—not just social-login buttons. See `docs/account-activation-2026-09-12.md` for the concrete backend gates.
